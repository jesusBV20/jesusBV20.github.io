---
title: "Geometric control on SO(3)"
summary: "Attitude control directly on the rotation group: no Euler-angle singularities, no quaternion ambiguities."
order: 4
---

Working directly on the Lie group SO(3) gives attitude controllers that avoid Euler-angle singularities and quaternion ambiguities. I use this to align robots with non-constant 3D vector fields, to extend source seeking to three dimensions, and to build guiding vector fields on the attitude manifold itself.
