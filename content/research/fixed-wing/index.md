Fixed-wing drones are efficient but hard to coordinate: they cannot stop, and their airspeed is tightly bounded. I implement and test our algorithms onboard real aircraft running the open-source [Paparazzi](https://github.com/paparazzi/paparazzi) autopilot - distributed formation flight that needs no speed actuation, guidance that keeps the trajectory in strong crosswinds, and fully autonomous aerobatics with guiding vector fields on SO(3).
