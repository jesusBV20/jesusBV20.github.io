Today was the annual CITIC Coffee event, where the entire researcher community of CITIC UGR gathered to present their work and engage in discussions regarding their last scientific advancements and potential collaborations. This year, our presentation focused on the work we showcased at ICRA 2024, titled "Behavioral-based circular formation control for robot swarms."
