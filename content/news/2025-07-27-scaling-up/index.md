Scaling up in aerial robotics is no easy task—not only in terms of algorithms, hardware, and software, but also in logistics, operations, and mission control.

Today, we successfully flew a squad of fixed-wing drones for the very first time, coordinating them in a fully distributed manner to maintain a 3D square formation. All computation was performed onboard, relying exclusively on relative situational awareness, without centralized coordination.

This flight marks an important step in our group toward scalable multi-agent aerial systems capable of operating cooperatively in complex environments.
