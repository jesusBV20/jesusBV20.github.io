The regional TV channel Canal Sur joined us during a flight day to record a science documentary about our current research in the field of imperfect robot swarms. They asked us a bundle of interesting questions, enabling us to provide didactic information to the general public about this fascinating field of robotics.

Huge thanks to the team at ConCiencia. Working with these highly skilled professionals has truly been a pleasure.

The science documentary is available online, only in Spanish :(
