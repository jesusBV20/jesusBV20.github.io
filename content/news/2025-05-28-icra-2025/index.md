I had a great time attending ICRA 2025 in Atlanta.

This year, I had the pleasure of presenting our latest research: "Inverse Kinematics on Guiding Vector Fields for Robot Path Following". It was a great opportunity to share our research and receive feedback from the robotics community.

Beyond the technical sessions (and outside them ^_^), I had the opportunity to reconnect with familiar faces — colleagues and friends from past conferences and summer schools — and to meet many brilliant new researchers working in the field.

Looking forward to the next one!
