Strong crosswinds? No problem. Watch as our fully autonomous fixed-wing drone navigates through strong crosswinds with remarkable stability and control. Thanks to our custom guidance system, the aircraft performs controlled lateral drift, maintaining its desired trajectory even when pushed sideways by wind.

This demonstration highlights a key capability for robust, real-world drone autonomy, especially in unpredictable weather conditions.
